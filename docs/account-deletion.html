Delete your PackRide account

Before publishing: replace [SUPPORT EMAIL] with a monitored public support address.

Delete in the app

  1. Sign in to PackRide.
  2. Open Profile.
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm.

Request deletion without the app

Email [SUPPORT EMAIL] from the address associated with your PackRide account with the subject “Delete my PackRide account.” We may ask you to verify ownership.

What is deleted

Your account, profile and photos, ride records and files, authored feed content, social connections, community memberships, invitations, RSVPs, location-sharing records, and active safety requests are deleted or disassociated.

Timing and retention

In-app deletion begins immediately. Support requests are processed after ownership verification. Limited records may be retained only when reasonably required for security, fraud prevention, legal compliance, or resolving the request, then removed when no longer needed.
