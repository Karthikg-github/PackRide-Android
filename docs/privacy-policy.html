PackRide Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 15, 2026

PackRide is a motorcycle ride-tracking application. This policy describes how we handle data.

Data we collect

  • Account: email (Firebase Authentication), display name, optional bike/city/avatar.
  • Location: GPS while you actively track a ride, lap session, group ride, Need Help share, or crash monitoring.
  • Sensor data: accelerometer for crash detection and optional lean/G-force on recorded rides.
  • Ride content: distance, speed, duration, GPX tracks, and optional feed posts you publish.
  • Microphone: live audio only when you deliberately join group voice chat. PackRide does not record voice calls.
  • Advertising data: identifiers and ad-related information processed by Google AdMob after the applicable consent flow.

How we use data

  • Provide tracking, group rides, safety alerts, and social feed features.
  • Sync rides and profile across devices via Firebase.
  • Provide maps and search through Google Maps and Places, live group voice through Agora, and advertising through Google AdMob.

Sharing

Follower and community sharing is off unless you enable it. Group rides and Need Help share live location only with the targets you choose. Crash information is sent only to linked emergency contacts and eligible active group riders. Feed posts are visible according to the social feature. We do not sell personal data.

Retention

Cloud data is retained while your account or content remains active. You can delete your account in-app (Profile, Delete account) or use the external deletion page. Limited records may be retained only where reasonably necessary for security, fraud prevention, legal compliance, or completion of a deletion request.

Your choices

You can control location audiences, stop tracking and crash protection, change Android permissions, modify advertising privacy choices where available, delete content, and delete your account.

Account deletion

Use Profile → Delete account in the app, or visit PackRide Account Deletion.
